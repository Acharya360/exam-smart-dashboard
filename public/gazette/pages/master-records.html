Statutory Gazette Approval & Release Workflow

Mandatory 5-stage institutional governance process required prior to the publication and legal sealing of any University Result Gazette.

1

Stage 1: Faculty Marks Submission & HoD Endorsement

Continuous Assessment (CA), Practical, and End-Semester Exam marks are entered, vetted against model answer keys, and digitally signed by the Course Instructor and Head of Department.

Custodian: School Faculty & HoD | Status: Completed & Locked
2

Stage 2: Statistical Moderation & Scrutiny Committee

The Moderation Committee reviews course-wise grade distributions, investigates standard deviation anomalies, and applies approved grace marks or moderation rules strictly as per University Ordinances.

Custodian: Scrutiny & Moderation Committee | Status: Minuted & Approved
3

Stage 3: Tabulation Register Verification & Secrecy Audit

Examination Secrecy Section cross-verifies all student enrollments, exam registration forms, backlog appearances, credits earned, SGPA/CGPA calculations, and prepares the master A3 Tabulation Register (TR Book).

Custodian: Dy. Registrar (Evaluation) | Status: Verified 100%
4

Stage 4: Board of Examinations & Evaluation (BoEE) Resolution

Formal presentation of the examination statistics, pass percentages, unfair means committee findings, and approval of the Gazette draft by the statutory Board of Examinations and Evaluation.

Custodian: Controller of Examinations | Status: Passed by Resolution
5

Stage 5: Vice-Chancellor Sealing & Gazette Publication

Final statutory assent by the Hon. Vice-Chancellor, sealing of the Tabulation Register with the University Crest, publication of the Official Result Gazette, and authorization of Grade Card dispatch.

Custodian: Vice-Chancellor & CoE | Status: Legally Published
